# Workspace Instructions for Treasures-Curse

## Particle FX
- All roadmap particle effects have been completed and implemented.
- When creating or modifying particle effects, follow established particle patterns in `scripts/items/` (e.g., `PlayerDeathEruptionEffect.gd`, `BloodSplatterEffect.gd`, `SparksEffect.gd`, `EnemyDissolutionEffect.gd`, `PedestalSocketEffect.gd`).

## Sound FX
- All roadmap sound effects have been completed and implemented (including chest opening, gold/gem/skull pickups, pedestal socketing, door interactions, and low health/stamina vitals audio).
- When creating or modifying audio or sound effects, follow established patterns (e.g., polyphonic or 3D player pooling for one-shots, stream looping with smooth volume fading for vitals/ambience, and `@export_range` properties for Inspector volume balancing).
